# Droppflow Company OS

Droppflow is a company operating system for owners and operators. Departments share one company brain. Agents prepare the work. People approve anything sensitive.

The product line is: **Run and grow your company from one intelligent operating system.**

Droppflow helps operate the company, and the Growth Engine helps find and convert the next customers. Agents prepare the work. People approve anything sensitive.

This repository is a populated prototype. It ships with three demo companies:

- Sinar Abadi Logistics
- Sentosa Dental
- Artha Construction

Switch companies in Settings. Approvals, chats, and dismissed pulse items stay in this browser.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the URL printed by Next.js. The default dev port in this environment is `4317` when started with `npm run dev -- --port 4317`.

## What you can walk through

- Overview and Company Pulse
- Command Center
- AI Workforce and each agent workspace
- Sales, Finance, Operations, Marketing, and Customer Service
- Approvals
- Activity and notifications
- Business Scan and the ROI calculator
- Owner WhatsApp preview, including a growth briefing and alert priority
- Growth Engine: overview, lead inbox, pipeline, outbound, marketing, social, campaigns, content, meetings, and analytics
- Morning brief, daily wrap-up, automations, agent builder, and integrations
- Onboarding checklist
- Light and dark mode

Growth prospecting, social publishing, outbound sending, and the integrations page are demo workflows. They are labeled as simulated or coming soon. Budget changes and customer messages wait for approval.

Voice uses the browser speech APIs when they exist, and falls back to typing.

Business Scan and the ROI calculator describe potential opportunity from the information provided. They do not promise savings.
